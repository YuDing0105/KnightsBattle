# Knights Battle

Current baseline: V0.4 Playtest.

- 8x8 match-3 board
- Four unit types and four visual levels each
- Approved 16 PNG unit icons are locked assets; do not redesign without explicit approval
- 3 match = 1 point; 4 match = 3; 5+ = 10
- Cascades multiply score and damage
- Matching grants XP and upgrades the corresponding unit Lv1 to Lv4
- Training Boss cannot die
- Track cumulative damage and highest single attack
- 18-move test round

Canonical asset backup: Google Drive / KnightsBattle.
